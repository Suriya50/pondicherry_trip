Who we are

A little escape from the everyday.

Pondicherry, also known as Puducherry, is a coastal destination famous for its promenade, beaches, French Quarter, cafés and relaxed atmosphere.

Pondicherry Trips is a travel website concept that helps visitors discover experiences around the city. Visitors can explore sample packages, compare trip ideas and send a booking request through a simple form.
